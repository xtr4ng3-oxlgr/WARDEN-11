# Ethics

WARDEN-11 is for authorized defensive work.

Allowed:

- your own domains,
- explicit client authorization,
- your own repositories,
- your own password hygiene lists,
- your own hash inventories,
- defensive reporting.

Not allowed:

- attacking third-party targets,
- brute force,
- credential theft,
- stealth scanning,
- bypass attempts,
- exploiting findings,
- using reports for harassment or extortion.

This project does not endorse misuse.
